How to read this registry

Each row is a series — a single, coherent measurement tracked over time or across geography. The Dimension column places it in one of eleven subject areas: wealth, income, employment, poverty, housing, education, criminal justice, business ownership, demographics, the historical slave trade, or geography. Each has its own page, and every series here links through to it.

Read the Source column to tell a measured series from a computed one. Where it begins with derived — derived (SCF), derived (BLS CPS), derived (TAST) — the series is calculated from other series as a ratio, a gap, or a share. Every other row is loaded directly from the original source named in that column.
