How to use these files

Every CSV opens with a header row. Most are wide — one row per year, one column per race group. Three are long, with one row per year-and-race: business_ownership_by_race.csv, unemployment_annual.csv and wealth_by_race_timeseries.csv. Column-level provenance (units, year span, source, notes) is recorded in data_dictionary.csv for the files it covers and in series_registry.json for the rest.

The series cover eleven dimensions: wealth, income, employment, poverty, housing, education, criminal justice, business ownership, demographics, the historical slave trade, and geography.

For the methodology behind each series — race-category harmonization, source vintage, and validation — see the Methodology & Provenance page. To reproduce the pipeline that builds these files from the original public APIs, see the Code & Reproducibility page.
